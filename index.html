Frequently asked questions

How long does the assessment take?

The assessment typically takes 15–20 minutes.

What will I receive after completing it?

A full personal report as a PDF with personalized practical recommendations. The SAR 400 package also includes a one-to-one consultation call to discuss your results.

How and when will I receive my results?

Your results are sent to the contact method you choose: email, WhatsApp or mobile. Timing: within 24 hours

How is my information kept private?

Your answers are used only to prepare your personal report and are not shared with third parties.

Is Basirah a psychological diagnosis?

Basirah is a self-reflection and personal development tool. It is not a psychological diagnosis and does not replace assessment or care from a qualified professional.
